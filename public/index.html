        Keyboard

        Playing notes

        The keyboard is a piano, like in FastTracker 2:

         2 3   5 6 7   9 0
Q W E R T Y U I O P   ← upper octave
 S D   G H J   L ;
Z X C V B N M , . /   ← lower octave

        F1–F8 octave · ` or < key off

        Hold several keys to play chords.

        Editing

        • Space edit mode on/off (stops playback)
        • Enter play song · Shift+Enter play pattern · Esc stop
        • ↑ ↓ ← → move · Tab next channel · PgUp/PgDn jump 16 rows
        • 0–9 A–F enter instrument / volume / effect values
        • Del clear field · Shift+Del clear cell
        • Ins insert row · Backspace delete row
        • Shift+arrows or drag to select · Ctrl+C/X/V copy/cut/paste
        • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+A select pattern
        • Ctrl+↑/↓ select instrument · Ctrl+←/→ previous/next order position
        • Alt+↑/↓ transpose selection or note
        • Click a channel header to mute, right-click to solo
